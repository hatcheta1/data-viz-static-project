## Description

I want to explore LGBT rights over time, and how changes in rights may relate to changes in the political landscape of a country. Alternatively, I'd like to look at colonial histories of countries, and see if that has an impact on the progressiveness of their LGBT policies (and social sentiment) today or over time.

## Data Sources

### Data Source 1: Equaldex: Gender marker can be legally changed

URL: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/right-to-change-legal-gender-equaldex

Size: 3650 rows, 4 columns

This dataset includes data from as early as 1950 to 2026, stating whether people in that country have the right to change their legal gender. The legal staus column includes the level of legality.

### Data Source 2: Equaldex: Marriage for same-sex partners

URL: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/marriage-same-sex-partners-equaldex

Size: 5042 rows, 4 columns

This dataset includes data from as early as 1950 to 2026, stating whether same-sex marriage is legal. However, the same-sex marriage column includes the highest level of recognized same-sex relationship in that country, such as "Civil union or other partnership" or "Unregistered cohabitation".

### Data Source 3: Comparative Political Data Set

URL: https://cpds-data.org/data/

Size: 1903 rows, 341 columns

This dataset has annual and political institutional data for 36 countries. It includes what makeup of the government is left-, center-, or right-leaning -- which I think is what I would focus on most. I am worried that the 36-country coverage will not be enough.

### Data Source 4: European overseas colonies and their colonizaers over time

URL: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/european-overseas-colonies-and-their-colonizers

Size: 88,747 rows, 4 columns

This dataset includes the colonial histories of each country for each year (as early as 1462) until 2022. It states what country they were colonized by, or if they were a colonizer in that year.
